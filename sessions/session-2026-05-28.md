## What I understood
- OLTP: designed for inserts, normalization matters
- OLAP: simpler queries, avoids joins, more columns, faster consults, better for reading, not the best for writing
- OLTP (Procesamiento de Transacciones en Línea) y OLAP (Procesamiento Analítico en Línea) 
- Star schema: centralized table with other tables surrounding it
- ETL(Extract Transform Load) source, extract, transform, load and target
